Roverhold Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game Roverhold (the “Game”) and roverhold.com, operated by Daniel Brand, handle information.

The Roverhold website

The website does not require an account and does not use advertising, analytics trackers, or cookies set by its own code. Images, fonts, and the gameplay trailer are served by the website rather than embedded from third-party media services.

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs and stores visitors' IP addresses for security purposes, even when visitors are not signed into GitHub. See the GitHub Privacy Statement for its data-handling practices.

If you contact Daniel Brand by email, your email address and the information you include are used to respond to your support or privacy request. Please do not send payment-card details or other information that is unnecessary for your request.

Information stored by the Game

The Game does not require you to create an account. Game progress, settings, purchases recognized by the Game, and your advertising privacy choice may be stored locally on your device. This local data remains on the device unless it is included in a backup provided by your device or platform provider. You can remove local data by clearing the Game's data or uninstalling the Game.

Optional rewarded advertising

The Game offers optional rewarded ads using Unity LevelPlay mediation, ironSource Ads, and Unity Ads. The advertising services are not initialized until you choose “Allow ads.” If you choose “No ads,” you can continue playing, but rewarded ads and their rewards are unavailable.

When advertising is enabled, these providers may collect or receive information such as:

  • device and advertising identifiers;
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • device, operating-system, language, network, and Game information;
  • ad impressions, views, clicks, and other interactions with ads; and
  • diagnostic and performance information.

This information may be used to provide, personalize, measure, and improve advertising; perform analytics; and prevent fraud or protect security and compliance. These providers may share information with advertising partners to perform these activities. Depending on where you live, targeted advertising can legally be considered a “sale” or “sharing” of personal information even when no money is paid for the information. Daniel Brand does not sell your personal information for money. These providers handle information under their own policies:

  • Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy (LevelPlay, ironSource Ads, and Unity Ads)

Your advertising choices

You can allow or decline advertising when the Game asks. You can change your choice later through Settings > Privacy Choices. Declining or withdrawing permission disables rewarded ads. Your mobile operating system may provide additional controls for advertising identifiers and tracking.

In-app purchases

Optional purchases are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play. Daniel Brand does not receive your full payment-card details. The Game receives purchase and entitlement information needed to provide or restore a purchase. Unity Gaming Services and Unity IAP help the Game connect to the applicable store. Unity and the store process information under their own privacy policies:

  • Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy
  • Apple Privacy Policy
  • Google Privacy Policy

Sharing and international processing

Information described above may be processed by the advertising, hosting, and store providers and their service providers. They may process information in countries other than the country where you live, subject to the safeguards described in their policies. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect users, rights, security, or the integrity of the Game.

Retention and deletion

Locally stored Game information remains until it is overwritten, cleared, or the Game is uninstalled. Third-party providers retain information according to their own retention policies. To ask a privacy question or request access to or deletion of information associated with the Game, contact Daniel Brand at the address below. Because the Game has no player accounts, additional device or transaction information may be needed to identify a record, and some requests may need to be completed directly through the relevant provider.

Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information handled in connection with the Game. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

Children

The Game is not directed to children under 13. Daniel Brand does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information in connection with the Game, please contact Daniel Brand so the matter can be investigated and appropriate action can be taken.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the Game or its services change. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page will be revised when changes are made.
