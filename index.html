What kind of game is Roverhold?

A top-down action survival game with automatic shooting, upgrade choices and base building. You control the rover, collect resources and survive enemy waves while your weapons handle the aiming.

How does moving the base work?

Your HQ and its defenses can follow your rover, stay in place, or move to a waypoint. Buildings are less effective while moving, so choosing when to stop matters.

What carries over between runs?

Your permanent base, unlocked content and Meta Upgrades help you start stronger. Power and upgrades bought during a run are temporary, giving you a fresh build to shape each time.

Can I keep playing after a level ends?

Yes. Finish the normal level and collect your completion, or enter optional Post Game for a tougher challenge and additional rewards.
